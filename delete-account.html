Delete Your FruBit Account

Last updated: 22 July 2026

This page explains how to delete your FruBit account and what happens to your data when you do. It applies to the FruBit consumer app (com.frubit.app), operated by FruBit.

How to delete your account from the app

You can delete your account at any time, directly in the app:

  • Open the FruBit app and sign in.
  • Go to the Profile tab.
  • Select Delete Account.
  • Confirm with your password.

Deletion takes effect immediately. You will be signed out and will no longer be able to sign in with that account.

How to request deletion without the app

If you have already uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email support@frubit.app from the email address registered to your account, with the subject line "Account deletion request". We will verify the request and delete the account. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, the following are permanently removed or anonymised:

  • Your name, email address and phone number — permanently replaced with anonymised placeholder values.
  • Your profile photo — removed.
  • Your saved wishlist and favourites — permanently deleted.
  • Your food preference filters — no longer associated with an active account.
  • Your ability to sign in — the account is deactivated and cannot be recovered or reactivated.

Any orders that are still active at the moment of deletion (pending, confirmed, being prepared, or ready for pickup) are automatically cancelled, and the reserved items are returned to the merchant's available stock.

What is kept, and why

  • Order and transaction records are retained in anonymised form. They are no longer linked to your name or contact details, but the underlying records must be kept to meet accounting, tax and consumer-protection obligations under Romanian and EU law, and to allow merchants to reconcile past payouts.
  • Reviews you have written remain visible on the merchant's page, but are anonymised and displayed as "Deleted User". This is so merchants' overall ratings are not retroactively distorted by account deletions.
  • Payment records held by Stripe. Card payments are processed by Stripe, our payment provider. FruBit never stores your full card details. Stripe retains transaction records under its own retention obligations — see the Stripe Privacy Policy.
Retention period. Anonymised order and transaction records are kept for the period required by applicable Romanian accounting and tax legislation, after which they are deleted. Because these records are anonymised at the point of account deletion, they can no longer be used to identify you.

Questions

For any question about this process or about your data more generally, contact support@frubit.app. See also our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
